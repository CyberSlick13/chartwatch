    Charts refresh from each platform about once an hour. Spotify and SoundCloud do not offer open chart data to new developers, so those tabs link to their own charts. Deezer is a separate service and is not a stand-in for either one. If a chart cannot load, the page shows made-up sample data with a tag at the top.
